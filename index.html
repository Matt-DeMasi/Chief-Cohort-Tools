⚙ Planning assumptions — drag sliders to update hiring timelines
These three numbers drive all the hiring dates below. Adjust them to match your current recruiting reality.
🎯 Alert threshold — when to start worrying
90%
At 90%, a chief with 95 max triggers a hire alert at 86 providers. Lower = earlier warning, higher = later warning.
📅 Weeks to recruit and hire a new chief
12wk
From posting the job to the new chief's first day. Typical range: 8–16 weeks.
📈 Months for a new chief to reach full caseload
6mo
After starting, how long until the new chief can take on a full cohort? The hiring date works backward from when we'll run out of space.
How this works: For each growing chief, we calculate when their caseload will hit the alert threshold. Then we count back the weeks to recruit + months to ramp — that's the date we need to start recruiting today for the new chief to be ready in time.
